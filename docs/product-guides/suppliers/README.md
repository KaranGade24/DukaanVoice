# Supplier management

[More tab](../more.md) · [All tab guides](../README.md)

Supplier management is reached through **More → Suppliers**. It keeps contact records and shows purchase/payment totals derived by the backend for the signed-in shop.

## Common tasks

- Find, add, edit, or archive a supplier: [Supplier records](supplier-records.md)
- Understand purchase totals, payments, and outstanding balances: [Supplier balances](supplier-balances.md)
- Record a payment: [Supplier payments](../purchases/supplier-payments.md)
- Start a purchase from a supplier: [Create a purchase](../purchases/create-purchase.md)

Supplier records are not shared across shops. The backend uses your authenticated account to resolve the shop and verify each supplier; the app does not let you choose or submit another shop’s ID.
