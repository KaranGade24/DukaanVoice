# Reports tab

[All tab guides](README.md)

## Current availability

The Reports tab currently shows **This section will be available soon.** It does not yet provide sales charts, downloadable reports, date filters, or export actions.

## Where to find useful information today

- For product, category, estimated stock value, and low/out-of-stock counts, open [Inventory](inventory/README.md).
- For a supplier’s purchase totals, payments, and outstanding balance, open **More → Suppliers**, then open a supplier. See [Supplier balances](suppliers/supplier-balances.md).
- For purchase records and payment history, open **More → Purchases** or **More → Supplier payments**. See [Purchase history](purchases/purchase-history.md).

The purchase invoice screen shares a DukaanVoice purchase summary. It is not a tax-reporting or accounting export and is not the supplier’s original invoice.

## What to expect

When reporting becomes available, check the selected date range, shop, and totals before relying on a report for business decisions. This guide will describe the actual filters and exports after those controls are added to the app.
