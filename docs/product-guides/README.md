# DukaanVoice mobile product guides

Use these guides to understand the tabs and complete the workflows currently available in the DukaanVoice mobile app. They are written for shop owners and staff and describe the app as it works today.

## Bottom tabs

| Tab | Guide | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | [Home tab](home.md) | Signed-in shop, language selection, navigation, and sign-out. |
| POS | [Point of sale](sales/point-of-sale.md) | Build a bill, accept cash/UPI/card, and decrement stock. |
| Inventory | [Inventory tab](inventory/README.md) | Product catalogue, categories, stock, alerts, and movement history. |
| Voice | [Voice tab](voice.md) | Current availability and how to handle shop changes until voice actions are implemented. |
| Reports | [Reports tab](reports.md) | Current availability and where to find existing summary information. |
| More | [More tab](more.md) | Supplier, purchase, supplier-payment, and language links. |

## Reading note

Voice and Reports currently display a **coming soon** message. Their guides do not describe planned capabilities as if they were available. Purchase totals, stock changes, supplier balances, and payment records are based on backend responses; a preview on a form is not a saved transaction.
