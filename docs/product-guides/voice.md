# Voice tab

[All tab guides](README.md)

## Current availability

The Voice tab is present in the bottom navigation, but it currently displays **This section will be available soon.** There is no recording button or voice-command workflow in this screen today.

You can still manage products and stock with the forms in [Inventory](inventory/README.md). Use those screens for any change that must be saved to your shop.

## Before using a voice workflow

Do not treat spoken instructions as completed stock or purchase changes. Until recording and confirmation controls are available in the app, no voice action is submitted by this tab. The screen does not request microphone permission or show a recording, processing, or result state.

## When the feature becomes available

Use the steps displayed in the app at that time, review any proposed product and quantity, and confirm changes only after checking them. Voice-created business actions should follow the same shop authorization and validation as the regular forms. This section will be updated when the mobile recording and backend command flow are implemented.
