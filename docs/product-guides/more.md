# More tab

The More tab includes a **Point of sale** link. It opens the same POS screen as the Home shortcut. See the [point-of-sale guide](sales/point-of-sale.md).

[All tab guides](README.md)

## What More contains

More is the navigation point for supplier and purchasing tasks, and for changing the app language.

| More option | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Point of sale** | Build and complete a cash, UPI, or card sale. |
| **Suppliers** | Keep supplier contact details and review their purchase totals and outstanding balance. |
| **Purchases** | Record deliveries, browse purchase records, and review a purchase summary. |
| **Supplier payments** | Record money paid to a supplier and review payment entries. |
| **Language selector** | Change the app labels between the languages currently offered. |

## Open a task

1. Tap **More** in the bottom tab bar.
2. Tap **Point of sale**, **Suppliers**, **Purchases**, or **Supplier payments**.
3. Use that screen’s add button, filters, and records to continue. The detailed workflows are in [Supplier management](suppliers/README.md) and [Purchases](purchases/README.md).

To change the language, choose one of the displayed language options. The selector currently offers English, Hindi, and Marathi. The active choice is visually selected.

## Sign out

Sign out from the button in the upper-right corner of Home. More does not contain a sign-out control.
