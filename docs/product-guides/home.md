# Home tab

[All tab guides](README.md)

## What you’ll find here

Home is the signed-in starting point for your shop. It shows the account name, the shop name, and the shop city saved for your account. It also gives you access to language selection and sign-out.

Inventory totals and stock tasks live in the **Inventory** tab. Supplier and purchase tasks are under **More**.

## Use Home

1. Check the shop name and city to make sure you are working in the right shop.
2. Choose a language with the language selector. The app’s translated labels update to that language.
3. Use the bottom tab bar to open Home, Inventory, Voice, Reports, or More.
4. To end your session, tap the sign-out button in the upper-right corner. The app returns to the sign-in screen.

## Example

If the shop card shows **Green Valley Store** and **Pune**, use the Inventory tab to check its products. Home itself does not display stock counts or sales reports.

## If shop details are missing

The shop card uses the shop information associated with your signed-in account. A dash means the city is not available in that information. If the shop name is also missing, contact your shop administrator or support before recording business activity.

## Data and access

Home reads the signed-in user and shop from the app’s authenticated session. Signing out clears the session through the existing authentication flow; it does not delete shop or inventory records.
