# Purchases and supplier payments

[More tab](../more.md) · [All tab guides](../README.md)

Open these tasks from **More**:

- **Purchases** lists records and starts a new purchase.
- **Supplier payments** records payments and lists payment history.
- A supplier’s detail page provides shortcuts to create a purchase or record a payment for an outstanding purchase.

## Choose a guide

- [Create and post a purchase](create-purchase.md)
- [Find, review, cancel, and share a purchase summary](purchase-history.md)
- [Record and review supplier payments](supplier-payments.md)

## Important distinction

A posted purchase is a saved business record. Posting also receives the listed quantities into inventory and may record an initial payment. The server is authoritative for totals, status, stock, and balances. The amount shown before posting is only a preview.

Purchase and payment posting require a transaction-capable MongoDB deployment. If the app reports that transactions are required, contact the administrator; do not create a second record to work around the error.
